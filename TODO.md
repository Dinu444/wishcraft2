# WishCraft Website — Delivery Outcomes

## 1. Brand, cover, and navigation — Complete
- Brand name is **WishCraft**; cover headline is **Digital Wish** and subheadline is **Crafted for Life.**
- Communicate that unlike traditional paper cards that get lost, thrown away, or delayed in the mail, WishCraft creates permanent, interactive digital memory keepsakes delivered precisely at 12:00 AM anywhere in the world.
- Cover section features artwork file `port.png` with an online fallback URL. Position “Digital Wish — Crafted for Life.” elegantly in the lower portion of the image.
- Blend the bottom of the cover image smoothly into the next section with gradient shading and no harsh cut-off lines.
- Header displays **WishCraft.** in bold text with no box icon before the name; include links for Home, Agency, Features, Our Works, AI Assistant, and Contact, plus a **Get Started** button.

## 2. Agency overview, card preview, candle, and marquee — Complete
- Main title: **Animated Card Agency.** Subtitle explains instant 12:00 AM delivery, zero distance limits, and interactive memories.
- Floating stat blocks display **240+ — Custom Wish Cards Designed**, **99.8% — On-Time 12:00 AM Delivery**, and **0 km — Distance Delay Guarantee**.
- Centerpiece displays a live card frame preview at `wishcraft.app/wish-9082`, titled **Happy Birthday, Sarah! 🎂**, with a tap-to-blow birthday candle demo.
- Right-side copy emphasizes **100% interactive cards** with sound effects, scratch cards, secret voice notes, and photo vaults. **Explore Designs →** scrolls to the portfolio section.
- An infinitely looping marquee displays: **✦ Birthdays ✦ Weddings ✦ Mother's Day ✦ Graduations ✦ Christmas ✦ New Year ✦ Father's Day ✦ Anniversaries.**

## 3. Interactive features — Complete
- Show six core capabilities: **Blow Out Candles** (interactive flame with blowing/tapping detection and extinguishing feedback); **Scratch Card Reveal** (scratch layer revealing secret messages, promises, or gift vouchers); **Photo Memory Vault** (interactive polaroid stacks, memory slideshows, and galleries); **Custom Music & Voice Notes** (background music integration plus personal voice-message player); **Celebration FX** (confetti explosions, rising balloons, floating hearts, and party poppers); and **12:00 AM Midnight Delivery** (zero-delay guarantee to surprise loved ones on time).

## 4. Work showcase / portfolio — Complete
- Include three dedicated, future-update-ready holders: **Sarah's 21st Birthday** (Candle Blow • Voice Note • Photo Vault) with a **Preview Demo** button; **Alex & Elena's Union** (Romantic Track • Scratch Reveal) with a **Preview Demo** button; **To Mom, With Love** (Polaroid Stack • Letter Scroll) with a **Preview Demo** button.

## 5. WishCraft AI Concierge — Complete
- Provide an interactive chat UI with **WishCraft Assistant** status and a live online indicator. Chat history scrolls smoothly with an invisible scrollbar while mouse and touch scrolling remain enabled.
- Clicking a preset question appends its question bubble, shows **WishCraft AI is typing...** for one second, then streams the answer into chat. Preset questions stay visible and clickable after use, and can be selected repeatedly.
- Preset Q&A must include these exact questions and answers:
  1. **“How long does it take to make a card?”** → Under 24 hours with priority for midnight delivery.
  2. **“How is the card delivered?”** → Sent via a private web link over WhatsApp or Email.
  3. **“Are these fully customized?”** → 100% custom-built with photos, music, names, dates, and secret notes.
  4. **“Do I need an app to open it?”** → No apps required; opens in any browser on mobile, tablet, or PC.
  5. **“How do I pay for my card?”** → Digital bank transfers and mobile payments after reviewing the draft.
  6. **“Can I add music & voice notes?”** → Yes, custom background music and recorded voice notes are fully supported.

## 6. Contact and social hub — Complete
- Include a direct WhatsApp order link box with subtitle.
- Include social buttons for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
- Inquiry form includes **Name**, **Occasion Selection** with Birthday, Wedding, Mother's Day/Father's Day, Graduation/Anniversary, and Custom Celebration; a **Message/Requirements** text box; and a **Send Inquiry Request** button.

## 7. Motion, responsive behavior, and accessibility — Complete
- Tapping or blowing out the central candle extinguishes the flame, updates the status message, and launches a multi-colored confetti explosion across the full viewport.
- Support smooth page scrolling, button hover lifts, and subtle pulse animations on status elements.
- Make the interactions usable on touch and mouse devices and layout adaptively across mobile and desktop.
